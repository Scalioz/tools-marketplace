What Is Guideline Value?

Guideline Value (also called Circle Rate or Ready Reckoner Rate) is the minimum property value set by the Tamil Nadu Registration Department for stamp duty calculation. If you buy a property for less than its Guideline Value, stamp duty is still calculated on the Guideline Value. Check current Guideline Values at tnreginet.gov.in for any specific locality.

Stamp Duty Concessions in Tamil Nadu

  • Women buyers - 2% concession on stamp duty for properties registered in a woman's name (property value up to Rs.25 lakhs)
  • Affordable housing - Reduced rates for PMAY-eligible properties under Rs.45 lakhs with carpet area below 60 sqm
  • Agricultural land conversion - Different rates for NA patta conversion before registration

Registration Process in Tamil Nadu

  1. Book appointment on tnreginet.gov.in
  2. Pay stamp duty online or at designated bank
  3. Prepare documents: sale deed (2 copies), EC (Encumbrance Certificate for 13 years), patta and chitta, ID proof of both parties
  4. Appear at Sub-Registrar Office with original documents on appointment date
  5. Biometric authentication of buyer and seller
  6. Receive registered document same day or within 2-3 working days

Encumbrance Certificate - Critical Verification Step

The Encumbrance Certificate (EC) shows all registered transactions on a property for a specified period. Obtain EC for the last 13 years (or since first registration) before buying any property. A clear EC confirms no loans, liens, or disputes on the property. Obtain from tnreginet.gov.in (online EC) or Sub-Registrar Office. Cost: Rs.100-300. Never skip this step.
