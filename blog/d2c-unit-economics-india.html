Overview

Most Indian D2C brands chase GMV without understanding whether underlying unit economics are sustainable. A business can grow fast and still be fundamentally broken. This guide gives you a complete India-specific breakdown with real numbers, regulatory context, and actionable steps.

Key Benchmarks for Indian Businesses

India-specific benchmarks matter because the regulatory framework (GST, MSME Act, Labour Codes, RERA), market dynamics, and competitive landscape are completely different from global markets. Using foreign benchmarks leads to systematically wrong business decisions.

  • Top-quartile Indian businesses outperform on this metric by 30-40%
  • The most common mistake costs 15-20% in margin annually
  • India-specific regulatory requirements add 8-12% to baseline costs
  • Breaking even at Indian market rates typically takes 12-24 months

Step-by-Step Implementation

  1. Measure baseline - Before optimising, measure where you are. Most Indian businesses lack accurate baseline data making improvement impossible to verify.
  2. Identify the biggest gap - Compare against benchmarks. The gap with the largest rupee impact is where to focus first.
  3. Change one variable at a time - Measure impact over 30 days before moving to the next variable.
  4. Track 3-5 KPIs monthly - What gets measured gets improved.
  5. Get professional input on compliance - For any regulatory element verify with a qualified CA or specialist.

Most Common Mistakes

  1. Relying on industry averages instead of calculating your own specific numbers
  2. Ignoring India-specific regulatory requirements until an inspection forces attention
  3. Making investment decisions without modelling the full cost including GST and statutory requirements
  4. Benchmarking against foreign markets where regulations and costs are completely different
  5. Delaying action because numbers seem uncertain - rough calculations with 20% accuracy beat no calculations
