Scalioz is the brand. Seociate Digital Business Solutions is the legal entity. We are a Chennai-based digital business solutions company helping Indian SMBs make better decisions through technology, data, and expert advisory.

Why We Built This

Most business tools available online are built for the US or UK market — they use dollar amounts, foreign tax structures, and completely irrelevant benchmarks for Indian businesses. An Indian restaurant owner searching for a food cost calculator gets results in USD with 8.875% New York sales tax.

We built Scalioz Tools to fix that. Every single calculator, estimator, and analyser on this platform is:

  • Built using Indian regulatory frameworks (GST, TDS, PF, ESI, RERA, FSSAI, MSME, and more)
  • Benchmarked against real Indian industry data — not US or UK averages
  • Priced in Indian Rupees (₹) with GST implications included
  • Relevant to Indian business contexts — cloud kitchens, Zomato/Swiggy economics, Mudra loans, stamp duty by state
  • Reviewed by Indian Chartered Accountants, lawyers, and industry practitioners
