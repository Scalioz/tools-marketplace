Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

Seociate Digital Business Solutions ("Scalioz", "we", "our") operates tools.scalioz.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in compliance with India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and email address when you contact us or subscribe
  • Business information entered into our calculators and tools
  • Payment details (processed by Razorpay — we do not store card data)
  • WhatsApp messages when you contact our support numbers

Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address and browser type for security and analytics
  • Pages visited and tool usage patterns via Google Analytics
  • Device type, operating system, and screen resolution
  • Referring website and search terms

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to our tools and services
  • To process payments and send receipts
  • To provide customer support via WhatsApp and email
  • To send service updates and new tool announcements (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our tools based on usage patterns
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing (governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy)
  • Google Analytics — for website analytics (anonymised usage data)
  • Law enforcement — when legally required under Indian law

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, access controls on our systems, and regular security reviews. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

We use cookies for: session management, Google Analytics (can be blocked via browser settings), and remembering your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser — some tool features may not function correctly without them.

6. Your Rights (DPDPA 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erase your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise your rights, email info@scalioz.com with "DPDPA Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations (typically 7 years for financial records under Indian law), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Children's Privacy

Our tools are designed for business use and are not intended for persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or prominent notice on our website. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
